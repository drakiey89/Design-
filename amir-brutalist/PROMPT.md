# AMIR Renal & Transplant Center — Brutalist Photorealism Prompt

Reinterprets the AMIR 15-level tower (clinical podium L2–5, ward tower L6–15, pointed-arch
entrance, أمير signage) as a massive board-formed concrete brutalist building.

## Master prompt

```
Hyperrealistic architectural photograph of the AMIR Renal and Transplant Center reimagined
as a monumental brutalist hospital: a 15-storey ward tower rising from a heavy 4-storey
clinical podium, entirely cast in raw board-formed béton brut concrete.

ARCHITECTURE & GEOMETRY: deep-set cantilevered window boxes stacked in a staggered grid,
chamfered vertical fins casting rhythmic shadows, a colossal pointed Islamic arch carved
into the tower's central spine as a negative void, recessed geometric mashrabiya screens
made of precast concrete hexagons and eight-point stars, layered horizontal slabs and
hovering sky-gardens, monolithic pilotis at ground level, interlocking cubic volumes,
board-marked cylindrical stair cores. The Arabic word "أمير" deeply incised into the
concrete above the entrance, and "AMIR" cast in raised concrete letters on the podium.

RAW TEXTURES: visible timber plank imprints with wood grain, form-tie holes in a strict
grid, honeycombing and aggregate exposure, cold joints between pours, rain streaks and
dark weathering stains running below sills, faint efflorescence, moss in shaded crevices,
contrasting with warm bronze window frames and smoked glass.

HUMAN SCALE: a nurse in teal scrubs walking across a wide concrete forecourt, an elderly
patient in a wheelchair accompanied by family in traditional baju kurung and songkok, a
doctor in a white coat pausing under the pilotis — all small against the building to
emphasise its monumentality, slight motion blur on walking figures.

LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE: golden hour, low raking sunlight grazing across the board-formed
surface to reveal every plank line, long deep shadows from the fins, warm interior glow
from wards above, humid tropical haze, scattered cumulus clouds, a shallow reflecting
pool in the foreground mirroring the tower, a few palm trees softening the base.

CAMERA: shot on Phase One XF IQ4 150MP with Rodenstock HR Digaron-S 32mm tilt-shift
lens, perspective-corrected verticals, low-angle three-quarter view, f/11, 1/125s,
ISO 100, tripod, natural colour grading, high dynamic range, ultra-detailed 8K,
architectural photography in the style of Hélène Binet and Iwan Baan.
```

## Negative prompt

```
cartoon, CGI look, plastic, smooth painted concrete, oversaturated, distorted verticals,
warped text, extra limbs, deformed faces, cluttered signage, low resolution, watermark
```
