# AMIR Renal & Transplant Center — Cinematic Video Shot List

Source frame: the AMIR render (cream stone tower, teal glazing, pointed-arch spine with أمير,
5-storey podium + 10-storey ward tower, palm-lined drop-off, fountain, ambulance bay,
golden-hour sky). Site: Lot PT 3095, Pulau Pinang.

Use the render as the **start frame** (image-to-video) for every clip. Tested-friendly models:
Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo, Runway Gen-4, NVIDIA Cosmos (NIM). 16:9, 24 fps, 5–10 s per clip.

**Global lock (paste into every prompt):**
> Keep the architecture, façade materials, cream stone, teal glazing, pointed-arch spine,
> أمير signage, landscaping, lighting and colour grade exactly as in the reference image.
> No new buildings, no morphing, no warping text. Photorealistic, stable gimbal, smooth
> ease-in/ease-out motion, natural motion blur, 24 fps, shot on DJI Inspire 3 / ARRI Alexa 35.

**Negative:** morphing geometry, flickering windows, melting text, jitter, fisheye, cartoon, CGI sheen.

---

## Shot 1 — Drone flyover reveal (8–10 s)

> Cinematic aerial drone shot. Start low and close above the palm trees and fountain in the
> foreground, camera tilted down at the drop-off. Drone rises slowly and steadily while
> pushing forward, tilting up to reveal the 5-storey podium, then the full 15-level tower
> and the green roof terraces. Continue ascending over the podium roof garden, finishing in
> a high three-quarter top-down view of the whole 2.5-acre site and the tower crown with
> mashrabiya screens, golden-hour light raking across the façade. Slow, weighty, stable,
> constant speed. [Global lock]

Camera: DJI Inspire 3, 24mm, f/5.6, 1/50s (180° shutter), ISO 100, ND filter, D-Log.

## Shot 2 — Façade hero (6–8 s)

> Slow lateral dolly/truck shot parallel to the main façade at ground eye level, 1.6 m
> high, moving left to right past the palm trees. The pointed-arch entrance and the
> AMIR RENAL AND TRANSPLANT CENTER lettering stay sharp and centred at the end. Gentle
> parallax between palms and stone façade, fountain water moving, a few visitors and a
> nurse walking in, ambulance parked at the emergency bay. [Global lock]

Camera: Alexa 35, 35mm anamorphic, T2.8, 1/48s, ISO 800, on slider/gimbal.

## Shot 3 — Walkthrough (8–10 s)

> Continuous steadicam walkthrough. Camera glides forward through the arched glass entrance
> into the main lobby: double-height space, warm cream stone, teal accents, mashrabiya
> screens casting geometric light patterns on the floor, registration desk with staff,
> patients and families seated, wayfinding signs to Dialysis Unit, A&E, Pharmacy. Camera
> keeps moving past the café toward lifts and a glass atrium looking up to the ward
> floors. Interior design follows the exterior language. Smooth, human walking pace,
> no head bob. [Global lock for exterior elements seen through glass]

Camera: Alexa 35, 21mm, T2.8, 1/48s, ISO 1280, steadicam, practical warm interior light.

## Shot 4 — Atmosphere change (same composition, 6–8 s)

Same camera move as Shot 1 or 2; only weather/time changes. Pick one per clip:

| Variant | Atmosphere line (replace golden hour) |
|---|---|
| A. Tropical monsoon | Heavy Penang monsoon rain at dusk, wet reflective forecourt, rain streaks in the air, lightning far behind, every ward window glowing warm. |
| B. Blue hour / night | Deep blue hour after maghrib, façade uplit, arch spine and أمير sign glowing, car light trails at the drop-off, stars emerging. |
| C. Misty dawn / Subuh | Soft pre-dawn fog, cool lilac light, silhouette of the tower emerging through mist, first sunlight touching the crown. |
| D. Ramadan night | Night scene with lanterns along the drive, families arriving, crescent moon above the tower, warm festive glow. |

> [Atmosphere line]. Architecture, materials, composition and camera path identical to
> the reference — only sky, weather and lighting change. [Global lock]
