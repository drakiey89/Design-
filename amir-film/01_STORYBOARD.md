### Motion rules (all shots)
- Movement is physically plausible, with ease-in and ease-out and constant speed in the middle. No drone spins, whip pans, speed ramps or crash zooms.
- Verticals stay parallel on every architectural frame; use tilt-shift or a post keystone fix.
- The camera never passes through walls, people or equipment. Scene changes inside the building are **motivated cuts**.
- Where only a still exists, use 2.5D parallax (depth-map projection), at most 3% scale change per second.
- Particles (dust in light shafts, haze) stay subtle and only appear where the light motivates them.
