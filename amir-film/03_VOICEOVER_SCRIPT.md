### Language check (in line with the brief)
- The script uses future and conditional wording throughout: *envisions, imagines, seeks, designed*. Nothing says the hospital is open, approved or built.
- It names no bed count, floors, costs, returns or regulatory status.
- It makes no clinical-outcome claims. "Pursuit of excellence in transplantation" states an aim, not a result.

### Recording spec
WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit, mono. Record each line as a separate take with 2 s of room tone.
Deliver dry (no effects). Mix at −16 LUFS integrated for presentation use, with dialogue peaks at −6 dBFS or below.
