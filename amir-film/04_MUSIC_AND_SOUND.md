# 04 — Music and Sound-Design Plan

## Music: a 90-second cue in five movements
License a track (Musicbed, Artlist or Epidemic: search "cinematic piano orchestral hopeful
institutional") or commission a 90 s bespoke cue. **Key: D major or B♭ major. Tempo: about 72 BPM.**

| TC | Movement | Instrumentation | Level under VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:10 | Opening | Solo felt piano, sparse; a soft string pad from 00:04 | −24 dB |
| 00:10–00:22 | Architectural reveal | Warm low strings join and the melody opens out; a gentle swell into 2.2 (the arch) | −22 dB |
| 00:22–00:34 | Arrival | Piano motif returns, lighter, with a warm cello line | −24 dB |
| 00:34–01:02 | Clinical sequence | Subtle rhythmic pulse (muted piano ostinato, soft pizzicato, light synth bed) at a steady pace; no percussion hits | −25 dB |
| 01:02–01:14 | Recovery | Pulse drops out; intimate strings and piano | −26 dB |
| 01:14–01:26 | Landscape and vision | Full strings and a soft brass pad build to an uplifting crescendo peaking at 01:22–01:25 | −20 dB, dropping to −24 under VO 7 |
| 01:26–01:30 | Resolution | Crescendo resolves to a single sustained piano chord with a natural decay to silence at 01:30:00 | −22 dB |

Duck the music by 4–6 dB under each VO line with a 300 ms attack and 600 ms release.
No musical stinger on the logo; let it resolve quietly.

## Sound design (subtle, never louder than the music)
| Scene | Layers |
|---|---|
| 1 | Distant morning city, birds (bulbul, oriental magpie-robin), faint sea wind |
| 2 | Light breeze through palms, fountain water, distant traffic, footsteps on stone (2.3) |
| 3 | Automatic door slide (3.1); lobby murmur in Bahasa and English; soft footsteps; gentle ambient air |
| 4 | Quiet clinic room tone, a soft conversational murmur (unintelligible), the gentle mechanical hum of dialysis machines; **no alarms** |
| 5 | Low HVAC and laminar-flow hush, quiet equipment hum; a single soft monitor tone at most, never an alarm |
| 6 | Morning room tone, distant birds through glass, a teacup set down in the lounge |
| 7 | Garden ambience, leaves, birdsong, wind chimes (very low) |
| 8 | Evening crickets and cicadas, distant soft traffic, fading under the final chord |

## Mix targets
- Presentation master: −16 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dBTP, AAC-LC 320 kbps, 48 kHz stereo.
- Broadcast or event variant: −23 LUFS (EBU R128) if it will play through venue AV.
- Deliver stems: VO, music, SFX, and the full mix.
