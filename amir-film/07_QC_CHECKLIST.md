# 07 — Final Quality-Control Checklist

Sign off each line before a stakeholder screening. Reviewer: ________ Date: ________

## Timing and technical
- [ ] Duration is exactly 00:01:30:00 (2,250 frames at 25 fps). Check with `ffprobe -show_entries format=duration`.
- [ ] Resolution is 3840×2160, square pixels, progressive, 25 fps constant frame rate.
- [ ] Codec is H.264 High (or H.265 Main10), Rec.709, at least 40 Mbps; audio AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo at 320 kbps or more.
- [ ] The clean master has **no** burned-in subtitles. The SRT is delivered separately and its timings match the VO.
- [ ] The first frame fades from black and the last 2 s hold the end card; no black flash at the end.

## Architecture and continuity
- [ ] The same building appears in every exterior shot: massing, arch spine, crown, podium steps and landscape all match A01.
- [ ] No invented wings, extra storeys, or a different façade between shots.
- [ ] No level numbers, bed counts or floor dimensions appear on screen.
- [ ] Verticals are true on every architectural frame; no keystone lean.
- [ ] Visitor arrival, emergency/ambulance access and service routes read as separate.
- [ ] No warping, flicker, swimming textures or melting window grids (check at 100% zoom, frame by frame on moving shots).

## Clinical credibility
- [ ] Equipment is plausible: dialysis machines beside chairs, CT gantry, theatre lights and booms, ICU monitors.
- [ ] No surgery in progress, no blood, no alarms, no distressed patients.
- [ ] Staff wear correct attire for each zone (scrubs, masks and gowns in theatre).
- [ ] Infection-control cues are present (hand-hygiene stations, clean surfaces).

## People
- [ ] Anatomy is correct: hands, faces, gait. Motion speed is realistic.
- [ ] No frozen "statue" people inside moving shots.
- [ ] Representation is respectful and authentically Malaysian, and includes elderly patients and a wheelchair user.

## Language, claims and brand
- [ ] Spelling: AMIR RENAL & TRANSPLANT CENTER · Al Aafiyah Group · Compassion. Precision. Care.
- [ ] No language implying the hospital is operational, approved or constructed (VO, titles, signage in shot).
- [ ] The disclosure appears at the open and at the end card.
- [ ] The أمير wordmark matches the approved vector; no AI-generated Arabic is visible.
- [ ] No political messaging and no exaggerated superlatives.
- [ ] Religious authorities' sensitivities reviewed (dress, prayer facilities, imagery).

## Audio
- [ ] VO is clear, with no clipping or mouth clicks; music sits under the VO with ducking.
- [ ] Loudness is −16 LUFS integrated (presentation) and true peak −1 dBTP or lower.
- [ ] No intrusive medical sounds; the ambience is subtle.
- [ ] Music resolves naturally by 01:30:00.

## Rights
- [ ] Music licence covers corporate and investor presentation use, worldwide, in perpetuity.
- [ ] Voice talent or AI-voice licence covers commercial use.
- [ ] Commercial-use terms of the AI generation platform have been checked.
